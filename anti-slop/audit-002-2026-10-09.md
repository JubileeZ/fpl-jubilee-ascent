## Numbered findings

1. **P1 / MEDIUM: Put deadline and decision summary where manager starts.** C-3, R-20, R-31: inspected planner leads with full squad rather than facts needed for deadline decision. Only Start: GW6 · upcoming deadline visible; absolute time, timezone and last refresh absent from inspected planner. Costs and scores below tall XI plus bench. Decide compact readiness summary: deadline, data age, active policy, captain, hits, bank, transfer action and legality. Missing metadata is evidence gap, not proof that data are stale.

2. **P1 / MEDIUM: Explain policy choice against holding squad over same horizon.** C-3, R-31: recommendation lacks visible reasoning for tradeoff. No Hit beats Optimal on displayed immediate scores, but horizon benefit and roll baseline unavailable in inspected planner. Decide common comparison metrics, hold baseline, horizon totals and hit justification. Preserve distinction between lineup projection, Expected GW Score and solver objective.

3. **P2 / HIGH: Distinguish selected policy from active draft.** R-27, C-4: pending/current state lacks explicit explanation. Selecting No Hit leaves Optimal headline until Use selected scenario; strategist and deadline reviewer independently observed. Decide preview versus adoption, explicit active/preview labels and protection for manual edits. Control has real behavior; not a dead-button claim.

4. **P2 / MEDIUM: Translate player risk and fixture detail into manager language.** C-3, R-31: raw values carry no visible meaning for first-time manager. Availability a and d; João Pedro Knee injury - 75% chance of playing available only inside inspector. Fixture cell combines opponent/home-away with unexplained decimal; component columns use xp_* identifiers. Decide plain availability labels, card-level risk summary, separate difficulty label and optional technical detail.

5. **P2 / MEDIUM: Connect scouting comparison to explicit planner preparation.** C-3, R-20: current composition requires memorizing and re-searching target. Sequential dialogs work; no visible side-by-side comparison, shortlist or target-handoff action in tested path. Decide comparison presentation and explicit reviewed handoff preserving separate What-If state and saved drafts.

6. **P2 / HIGH: Validate chip conflicts before experiment submission.** R-27, C-4: invalid-looking configuration has no local guidance. Wildcard GW8 plus Bench Boost GW8 simultaneously selectable; Run advanced solve remains available; no warning visible. Decide immediate validation and distinction between one fixed schedule versus alternatives. Backend rejection and solver handling untested; do not assert invalid solve succeeds.

7. **P2 / MEDIUM: Explain strategy controls at point of choice.** C-3, R-31: visible technical weights have no units or examples. Decay 0.85, Free Transfer value 1.50, Bank value 0.08 and bench weights 0.21/0.06. Methodology describes projections but does not answer planner policy questions. Decide plain meanings, impact examples, risk presets and contextual links. Empty wildcard research search is not itself a defect.

8. **P3 / HIGH: Make lifetime and recovery of advanced setup explicit.** R-27, C-4: reload loss lacks advance session-only warning. WC/BB selections reset after reload; saved draft recovery works. Decide session-only disclosure versus saved experiment setup and recovery behavior. One View preference could not be saved warning requires independent reproduction before root-cause ticket.

Antislop tiers reflect cited rule families; P1/P2/P3 reflect FPL user impact. Findings are concerns for decision tickets, not claims of exhaustive design compliance.
