# Native replay: known answers and source boundary

Examples specified before implementation. Money units = £0.1m. Initial fifteen acquisition cost 990, bank 10: capital 1000, not 1990. GW1 locked; next deadline has one FT.

- Bought at 50, market 53: sale 51; market 49: sale 49. Sell at 51 with bank 10, buy at 55: bank 6. Rebuy at 53 establishes acquisition price 53. Retained players keep acquisition prices. Free Hit restores original holdings and bank after scoring. [Price explanation](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2858775).
- With two FT and three ordinary transfers: hit 4; next FT 1. With two FT and no transfers: next FT 2 in 2023-24, 3 in 2024-25. Wildcard or Free Hit restores one FT in 2023-24; preserves two in 2024-25. [2023 launch](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/3568527), [2024 changes](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4058895).
- Starting eleven earns 40 including captain 6; captain increment 6, one excess transfer penalty 4: net 42. Captain did not play, vice scored 5: Triple Captain adds 10. Bench Boost adds all four bench scores. A player appearing in either DGW fixture is available for substitution purposes; points sum over both fixtures. Missing outcomes are unknown, never zero.
- 2025-26 GW15 transition tops FT up to five for GW16. [Launch article](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4362211) displays 19 July 2025, but later edits to its content cannot be excluded. Independent [19 July report](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/07/19/fpl-2025-26-extra-free-gameweek-16-transfers-ahead-of-afcon) corroborates announcement by that date; publication time unknown, so conservative as-of bound 20 July 00:00 UTC. [Official implementation report](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4461660) confirms effective timing.
- Goalkeeper goal: six points in 2023-24, ten in 2024-25 onward. Defensive contributions begin in 2025-26: two points per match at defender 10 CBIT or midfielder/forward 12 CBIRT; none for goalkeeper. [2024 scoring changes](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4059044), [2025 changes](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4362211).

2023-24 Wildcards: GW1–20 and GW21–38; [2024-25](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4209923) first GW1–19 and second GW20–38; 2025-26 four chips per half, first GW1–19, second GW20–38. [2024-25 Free Hit preservation example](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4059225); [official AFCON explainer](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4362102). Assistant Manager excluded from supported policy. 2025 assist definitions changed; BPS also changed in 2024. Official observed points are authoritative; no reconstruction of unavailable historical definitions. Historical card-only autosub eligibility remains unverified; reject ambiguous cases rather than importing later rules.

Current [official FAQ](https://fantasy.premierleague.com/help/faqs) allows an inherited over-limit squad after a real club transfer, requiring compliance on the next manager transfer. Historical applicability still requires dated evidence; the current financial primitive rejects such states rather than forcing a correction. Purchase eligibility is separate from continued ownership and must be admitted before an integrated replay.

## Supported contract and remaining integration

Only Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost and Triple Captain supported. Assistant Manager excluded. One chip per deadline; chip history and windows constrain later decisions. Shared calendar construction and causal calendar publication still require an admission adapter. Transition consumes admitted deadline squad/market metadata and complete official player-GW outcomes; caller must verify every fixture, including both DGW fixtures, before aggregation. Missing outcome or unknown autosub eligibility rejects transition. Zero-minute scoring/card-only cases reject pending historical evidence. Inherited over-limit clubs reject pending dated evidence; neither case triggers a forced transfer or zero substitution.

Free Hit restoration retains pre-decision purchase bases, market snapshot and bank. Before the next decision, refresh owned metadata from admitted next-deadline inputs; preserve acquisition bases. State includes rule identity, ownership, bank, FT, history, previous action/hit and parent hash. Each decision binds its pre-state hash and deadline; an immutable outcome batch binds season, deadline and source identity and may be shared across model states at that deadline. GW1 locks transfers but permits Bench Boost or Triple Captain. Transition returns gross, captain increment, hit and net separately. Solver, historical acquisition, and empirical admission remain separate work. Existing solver and legacy lineup helpers are not native replay implementations.

The optional `native_rules_version` Feature Contract column must be uniform and complete when supplied. The current product default remains unchanged when absent; this default is not evidence of 2026-27 native support. Native observed components require explicitly present fixture fields; native assists and bonus must come from official recorded values, not reconstructed using current definitions. Legacy callers without explicit context retain their existing behavior and are not admitted to native replay by these changes.
