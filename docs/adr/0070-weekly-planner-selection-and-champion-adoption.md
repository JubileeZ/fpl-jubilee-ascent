## Weekly Transfer Planner selection

- Rank Confirmed Champion and eligible frozen finalists by cumulative realized net FPL points (official points minus Hits) on same verified, contiguous settled Gameweeks. Compare independent replays from neutral GW1 anchor. Use highest scorer for next Transfer Plan on actual User Squad. Prefer Champion on tie with Champion or missing comparable evidence. Preserve explicit model override. Show model, comparison scores, and latest settled Gameweek in preflight.
- Ordinary points deficit changes weekly model choice. Keep trailing finalist eligible for later confirmation, including at GW15. Keep eligibility independent of `provisional_active`. Early-Harm or demonstrated mandatory guard failure can exclude finalist under registered rules. Calibrate irreversible harm decisions across repeated checks before expanding their cadence. Inconclusive guard blocks adoption while finalist remains eligible.

## Adoption trigger and approval

- Initial rollout: automate evidence collection, settlement checks, and existing GW15/GW30/GW38 formal evaluations. Design target: first verified full pass at any settled Gameweek from GW15 through GW38. Require statistical superiority to frozen Confirmed Champion plus required projection and segment safeguards.
- Compare current three looks, GW15/GW23/GW30/GW38, and weekly looks under null and improving-model scenarios before using new thresholds. Preserve serial dependence and shared Champion across finalists. Require season-wide false-promotion rate ≤5% across weeks and frozen finalists. Prefer weekly confirmation if it materially reduces time to confirmation while preserving GW38 power. Calibrate outside quarantined live validation season. Register approved protocol before accessing active-season evidence for slate. Retain current schedule until calibration passes and new protocol is registered.
- First verified passing result produces one email linking to protected GitHub Actions approval. Bind approval to immutable evidence bundle, bundle hash, and expected Confirmed Champion. After approval, re-verify bundle and current selection, run `adopt_champion`, and land selection plus adoption receipt through permitted branch workflow. Require fresh approval for changed evidence or Champion. Keep weekly serving updates independent of pending checkpoint evidence. No unattended Champion change.

## Single-user GitHub-native notification and $0 parity amendments (2026-11-21)

- **GitHub-native Adoption Review Issue default.** Single-user operations (`CONTEXT.md`) use one deduplicated `Adoption Review Issue` (`champion-adoption-review` label, assigned `JubileeZ`, hidden `<!-- adoption-request-id:<request_id> -->` marker) plus native `champion-adoption-approval` Environment email instead of requiring external `MAIL_PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_URL` credentials. External webhook providers remain optional.
- **Review Issue lifecycle and authority.** On adoption PR creation, comment PR link and head SHA while leaving issue open. On rejection, expiry, invalidation, or completed merge onto protected `main`, comment disposition and close issue. Issue comments or manual close carry zero approval authority; only `champion-adoption-approval` authorizes adoption.
- **Quiet routine weekly runs.** `collect`, `scorecard`, and non-passing `replay` runs persist to `validation-evidence` and Actions Job Summary without opening weekly scorecard issues. Open `Adoption Review Issue` only on verified pass awaiting human review.

Throwaway pilot repository may run public with zero secrets or personal data and be deleted after pilot completion; retired pilot App installation may move to production once deleted.

## Empirical rolling-MILP scale-free Early-Harm and power diagnostics amendment (2026-11-22)

- **Scale-free advisory `gw1_4_material_loss_limit`.** Across all `13` completed non-live `S-1`/`S-2` rolling-MILP trajectories (`EXT-01_1`–`EXT-05_1`), `13/13` show zero weekly delta at `GW1` and `GW2` from locked `GW1` opening transfers (`SD(sum_1..4) = 7.52` pts; `0/13` breached `-25`), while weekly Transfer Planner (`models/planner_selection.py`) reversibly switches to `Confirmed Champion` on any negative cumulative delta. Under `statistical_early_harm_mode="advisory"`, `gw1_4_material_loss_limit = 35.6` net FPL pts (`4 * 8.9`, [ADR 0071](0071-certified-solver-budget-and-milp-qualification-calibration.md) `cold_start`) logs to `advisory_reasons` without irreversible terminal exclusion; terminal Early-Harm exclusion restricts strictly to demonstrated mandatory projection guard `FAIL` (`0/20,000` terminal false exclusion, `upper_95 = 0.000150`).
- **Decoupled fixed-FPL-pts/yr power diagnostics and fresh-seed certification.** `backtesting/sparse_certification.py` (`manifest_version=2`, fresh preregistered seeds `21101/21102/21103`) decouples scale-invariant standardized `+0.40 SD` SNR efficiency gate (`>= 1/3`) from fixed-FPL-points/year empirical power scenarios (`+40/+60/+80` pts/yr under empirical `sigma_gw = 8.5` pooled non-zero active and `11.0` upper stress, locked-GW1/GW2 profile, `phi = 0.10`), recording single-season `38`-GW confirmation limits and `76`-GW two-season carryover diagnostics ahead of pre-`2027-28` summer-rollover carryover policy.
