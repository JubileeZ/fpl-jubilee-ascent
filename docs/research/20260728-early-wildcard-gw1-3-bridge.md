## What “settle” means by GW4/5

After ~3 GWs you usually know:

1. **Minutes** — who is actually N vs R (Iraola / Alonso / Maresca / Sage flux).  
2. **Pen takers** — Isak / Wright / McBurnie / new clubs.  
3. **Injury survivors** — WC-rest returnees (Saka/Rice path, Porro, etc.).  
4. **Price movers** — who rose £0.1–0.3 and who tanked.

You still will **not** know long-term form. Early WC is about fixing **role + fixture** mistakes, not finding the season winner.

## Price-rise problem — mechanics & mitigations

### Why your worry is correct

FPL sell price ≈ purchase + **floor(rises / 2)**.  
If Palmer goes £9.5 → £10.0 and you **don’t** own him, WC needs **+£0.5 cash**.  
If three targets each rose £0.3, you can be **£0.9 short** on a tight build — classic early-WC failure mode.

### Mitigations (ranked)

1. **Own the targets** you are most afraid of missing (Palmer / Saka / Isak / Haaland).  
2. **Front-load ownership of template risers** even if GW1–3 is only OK — value > perfect short-term XI.  
3. **Bridge with mid-price differentials** (£6.0–8.0) that *you* expect to rise: you capture ~half and fund WC.  
4. **Leave £0.5–1.5 ITB** only as buffer for *unknown* rises — not as primary plan.  
5. **Do not** park £15m+ in dead assets you will sell that are unlikely to rise (injured R-tier, blank-prone £4.5).  
6. Accept: some GW3 heroes you don’t own will be unreachable — WC is not omnipotent.

### Anti-patterns

| Mistake | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Full differential opener, zero template | Risers you need become unaffordable |
| Max ITB, thin squad | Misses rises; still can’t buy inflated names |
| Bridge = 5× expensive one-week punts | Sell-price friction + hits if WC delayed |
| Delay WC to GW8 “for more data” | Burns free-hit-like window; hits stack; WC1 still expires GW19 |

## When **not** to early-WC

- Your GW1 squad is already mostly A-layer and fixtures stay fine through GW8.  
- You need WC1 for a known blank/DGW cluster later in GW10–19.  
- Team value already wrecked (too many falls) — early WC still OK, but expectations lower.  
- You planned BB/TC for GW1–5 instead (rare) — chip congestion.
