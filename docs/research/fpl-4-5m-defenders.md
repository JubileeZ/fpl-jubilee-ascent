## Source synthesis

### Main candidates

- **Luke Shaw — Manchester United, £4.5m**: 38 starts; 3.0 points per start; 0.28 shots, 0.45 chances created, and 6.27 DefCon per 90. Article reports 23.1% ownership, highest among this price group. After Michael Carrick’s appointment, cited points per match rose to 3.9 across 17 matches, including one goal, seven clean sheets, six DefCon points, and three bonus points. First two fixtures against promoted sides drive early appeal; schedule worsens after Gameweek 3 and Champions League involvement adds future rotation context.
- **Tyrick Mitchell — Crystal Palace, £4.5m**: 36 starts plus two substitute appearances; 3.7 points per start; 0.53 shots, 0.80 chances created, and 6.84 DefCon per 90. Source says he scored 135 points, most among players in this article, with one goal, three assists, 12 clean sheets, and eight bonus points; only 12 DefCon points. Secure-value case depends on Pierre Sage retaining Oliver Glasner’s 3-4-2-1 wing-back structure. Palace rank fifth by first-six clean-sheet percentage in the cited Fixture Ticker. Source suggests pairing with a Coventry £4.0m defender to avoid Manchester City in Gameweek 2.
- **Joe Rodon — Leeds, £4.5m**: 33 starts plus two substitute appearances; 3.3 points per start; 0.55 shots, 0.27 chances created, and 8.31 DefCon per 90. Joint-most DefCon points among £4.5m defenders in 2025/26 at 18. Leeds reportedly kept six clean sheets in 20 matches from Gameweek 19 onward and have three home fixtures in the first five Gameweeks. Rodon also ranked joint-11th among defenders for headed attempts, with 15.
- **Michael Kayode — Brentford, £4.5m**: 37 starts; 3.1 points per start; 0.25 shots, 0.94 chances created, and 6.05 DefCon per 90. Created 34 chances, second-most for Brentford, and delivered 169 long throws into the box, a cited league high; only six DefCon points. Source sees him as the best cheap Brentford defensive option if Kristoffer Ajer is not nailed. Brentford had 10 clean sheets in 2025/26; Sunderland and Hull are cited as better Gameweek 3 and 8 targets.
- **Ola Aina — Nottingham Forest, £4.5m**: 18 starts; 3.7 points per start; 0.68 shots, 0.51 chances created, and 7.37 DefCon per 90. A preseason back-three trial under Glasner increases his appeal; source notes an assist against Vitoria de Guimaraes. Forest’s first four include Liverpool, Tottenham, and Aston Villa, so article suggests rotation with a Coventry defender. Jair Cunha is another Forest option but carries minutes risk if Ousmane Diomande arrives.
- **Ferdi Kadioglu — Brighton, £4.5m**: 34 starts plus three substitute appearances; 3.4 points per start; 0.80 shots, 0.60 chances created, and 4.89 DefCon per 90. Made the left-back position his own and started 34 league matches, but supplied only three attacking returns. Brighton’s cited late defensive improvement and early fixtures against Leeds, Coventry, and Sunderland support monitoring.
- **Lewis Dunk — Brighton, £4.5m**: 31 starts plus two substitute appearances; 3.2 points per start; 0.76 shots, 0.22 chances created, and 7.41 DefCon per 90. Recorded 16 DefCon points and 15 headed goal attempts, giving a set-piece route. New defenders and European Conference League play-offs create minutes and workload uncertainty.
- **Maxim De Cuyper — Brighton, £4.5m**: 17 starts plus 13 substitute appearances; 3.6 points per start; 0.80 shots, 1.96 chances created, and 3.98 DefCon per 90. Source credits stronger assist potential and occasional advanced positioning but does not regard him as nailed.
- **Matty Cash — Aston Villa, £4.5m**: 34 starts plus one substitute appearance; 3.4 points per start; 0.90 shots, 0.90 chances created, and 5.57 DefCon per 90. Villa’s top-scoring defender at 117 points, with three goals and three assists in 35 matches. Source notes long-range shooting, corners, and crossing; RMT ranks him first among £4.5m defenders for the first six Gameweeks. Fixtures improve after Brighton and Arsenal, with Hull, Forest, and Brentford cited.
- **Antonee Robinson — Fulham, £4.5m**: 17 starts plus five substitute appearances; 3.5 points per start; 0.36 shots, 1.30 chances created, and 8.05 DefCon per 90. Fitness and form limited his starts; Ryan Sessegnon competes for minutes. A Gameweek 37 penalty is a possible clue for a replacement penalty role after Raul Jimenez’s departure. Fulham’s Gameweek 6–8 run against all promoted teams is the main fixture case, while new manager and front-foot style add defensive risk.
